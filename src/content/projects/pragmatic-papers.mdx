# The Pragmatic Papers

The Pragmatic Papers is the news website and content management system for [Digital Ground Game](https://github.com/digitalgroundgame). As <mark>Technical Lead</mark>, I lead its development and keep shipping new features for writers and readers.

## My Role

- Maintain the site as its primary volunteer developer, shipping new features on a steady cadence.
- Review pull requests from other Digital Ground Game volunteers and give feedback that keeps code quality high.
- Make the architecture call on every addition so the site stays fast while the reading experience keeps getting richer.
- As a <mark>Deputy Director</mark> at Digital Ground Game, meet several times a week with writers, developers and leadership to keep Pragmatic Papers at the center of our digital media strategy.

## Agentic Development

Pragmatic Papers is where I stay on the cutting edge of <mark>agentic development</mark>. I run agentic loops to turn new feature requests around quickly, watch for the places where models make mistakes, and steer each change so it fits the architecture instead of piling on.

## Stack

- Front end built with <mark>Next.js</mark> and <mark>React</mark>, backed by <mark>Payload CMS</mark> on <mark>PostgreSQL</mark>.
- Editors publish articles, volumes, pages and media from a custom admin area.
- A database seeder loads mock articles, volumes, pages and media so local development starts with realistic content.
- UI components are documented in <mark>Storybook</mark> with accessibility tests, and end-to-end flows are covered with <mark>Playwright</mark>.
